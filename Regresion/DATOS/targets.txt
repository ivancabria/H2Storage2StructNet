   2025 targets: vc->0.040 kg/L and gc->5.5 wt. %
   Ultimate targets: 0.050 kg/L and 6.5 wt. %


The 2018 Toyota Mirai, a commercial vehicle with tanks storing 
compressed hydrogen at 70 MPa within a total volume of 122.4 L. 
The Mirai has a gravimetric capacity of 5.7 wt%, a volumetric 
capacity of 0.040 kg/L and a range autonomy of 312 miles (502 km). 
See: Broom DP, Webb CJ, Fanourgakis GS, Froudakis GE, Trikalitis PN, 
Hirscher M. Concepts for improving hydrogen storage in nanoporous 
materials. Int J Hydrog Energy 2019;44(15):7768-79
http://dx.doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhydene.2019.01.224
